# Audio and picture quality review

Verdict: technically complete, but not editorially perfect.

## Audio
- Complete original narration and order preserved. Source master remains unchanged.
- Source and MP4 integrated loudness: -20.4 LUFS; loudness range: 3.7 LU.
- Source true peak: +2.3 dBTP. MP4 true peak: +1.4 dBTP. Both exceed 0 dBTP and lack safe peak headroom. This indicates overload risk on playback, not proof of audible clipping at a particular moment.
- No limiter, normalization or gain adjustment was applied, following the instruction to preserve narration.
- Five decoded waveform comparisons correlate 0.9976–0.99998, with RMS ratios 0.9986–1.0013. This supports preserved timing and level at those positions; it is not a listening review of every spoken word.
- MP3 was transcoded to AAC for MP4 compatibility, so delivered encoded audio is not bit-identical to the source.

## Pictures
- 268 visual shots use only 35 distinct illustrations (37 generated). These are camera moves over still images, not moving human performances.
- Writing-room image reused 25 times, total 329.1 seconds. Dark-street image reused 26 times, total 323.6 seconds. Together, these two pictures occupy about 19% of the video.
- Median shot: 11.38 seconds. Fifteen shots exceed 25 seconds; longest is 38 seconds. Repetition is the main editorial weakness.
- Source images are approximately 1672x941, enlarged to 1920x1080 for delivery. The output is 1080p, but does not contain native 1920-pixel source detail.
- All source illustrations and 536 near-boundary frames were visually reviewed. Overt problems were checked, but exact face/object continuity, subtle anatomical issues and historical accuracy cannot be certified as perfect.
- 97.77% normalized script/ASR word matching supports timeline alignment. It does NOT mean 97.77% visual-semantic accuracy.

## Completed checks and missing signoff
PASS — Full-file decode, all 85828 frames, no detected black placeholders, no missing media, complete duration and continuous timeline.
PASS — Original master retained; no intentional speed, order, silence or volume changes.
FAIL — Safe peak headroom: true peaks exceed 0 dBTP.
FAIL — Nonrepetitive visual finish: only 35 illustrations across 57 minutes.
FAIL — Exhaustive human real-time audiovisual signoff: not performed. Automated full-file checking and source/boundary-image review do not replace watching and listening to the entire film.

Recommended improvement order: review and, with authorization, control audio peaks on a separate delivery copy; add more topic-specific illustrations; then complete a continuous human listening and semantic/continuity review. Preserve the original master.
