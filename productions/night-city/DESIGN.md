# Historical night reconstruction
## Style Prompt
Calm painterly realism depicting ordinary urban life in the Russian Empire, mainly a provincial city around 1820–1850. Modest wooden streets, plastered masonry shops, hand-worked iron oil lanterns, natural human anatomy and working postures. Low-key but readable firelight, moonlight and winter twilight. Slow camera movement and restrained dissolves. No captions, logos, cartoons, electric lighting or modern objects in early-period scenes. Clearly separate later gas/electric historical passages and contemporary comparisons.
## Colors
- #111b29: night sky and deepest shadows
- #435569: cool blue snow and ambient night
- #bb8549: warm oil lantern light
- #dfc89e: candlelit skin and plaster
- #544331: aged timber
## Typography
No on-screen narration text. Optional production notes use Noto Sans.
## What NOT to Do
No stick figures. No frantic cuts. No fabricated character dialogue. No changing narration. No modern props in early nineteenth-century settings. Do not claim still images contain generated physical human motion.
